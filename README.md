## About

I am a **Software Engineering graduate** and an **M.Sc. candidate in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science** at **Istanbul Aydın University**.

My work focuses on the intersection of **Artificial Intelligence**, **Data Science**, **Digital Twins**, **airline operations**, and **decision support systems**. I am especially interested in intelligent systems that do more than prediction: systems that simulate operational scenarios, evaluate constraints, explain trade-offs, and support human decision-makers in complex real-world environments.

Alongside my academic work, I have aviation-domain experience across **airline operations**, **crew planning concepts**, **revenue management**, **ancillary products**, **pricing**, **data analysis**, and **operational decision-making**.

## Research Direction

<div align="center">

```text
AI + Digital Twins + Airline Operations
        -> simulation
        -> regulatory constraint modelling
        -> robust disruption recovery
        -> explainable decision support
```

</div>

My master's thesis investigates an **Adaptive Digital Twin Framework for Robust Airline Crew Disruption Recovery under Operational Uncertainties and Regulatory Constraints**.

Core research themes:

- Digital Twin architectures for live operational state representation
- Crew management and airline disruption recovery
- What-if simulation and downstream impact analysis
- Regulatory constraint modelling and fail-closed decision logic
- Human-in-the-loop decision support for operational controllers
